## Purpose

Defines the process for handling delivery-related complaints: late
delivery, missing package, damaged package, wrong item, and delivery
partner issues.

## Late Delivery

1. Check current shipment tracking status.
2. If delayed beyond the promised window by less than 48 hours, provide an
   updated ETA and a goodwill discount code where applicable.
3. If delayed beyond 48 hours with no tracking movement, treat as a
   potential lost shipment and offer replacement or full refund.

## Missing Package (marked delivered, not received)

1. Ask the customer to check with neighbors/building reception and
   confirm the delivery address.
2. Request delivery partner proof-of-delivery (photo/signature) from the
   logistics dashboard.
3. If no valid proof of delivery exists, or evidence contradicts the
   customer's address, process a replacement or refund without requiring
   further proof from the customer.

## Damaged Package / Wrong Item

1. Request photos of the damaged item or incorrect item received.
2. Approve replacement or refund without requiring the customer to return
   the damaged/incorrect item for low-value orders (under the current
   threshold set by Operations).
3. For high-value orders, arrange a reverse pickup before issuing the
   replacement/refund.

## Delivery Partner Issues

Repeated complaints about a specific delivery partner (rudeness, failed
delivery attempts without contact) should be logged and escalated to the
Logistics Partner Management team on a weekly basis, or immediately if
complaint volume for a specific partner spikes.
