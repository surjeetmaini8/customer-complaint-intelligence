## Description

A known bug causes the app to crash when customers on certain older
Android OS versions (Android 9 and below) attempt to open the checkout
screen with more than 10 items in their cart.

## Status

Open - scheduled fix in the next app release (tracked internally as
APP-4471). Workaround available in the meantime.

## Workaround

Advise affected customers to:
1. Reduce the cart to 10 or fewer items before checkout, complete the
   purchase, then repeat for remaining items, OR
2. Use the mobile website (web checkout) instead of the app, which is not
   affected by this bug.
