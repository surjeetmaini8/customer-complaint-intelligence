## Q: A customer reports a transaction they did not make.

A: Treat this as high priority regardless of transaction amount.
1. Do not attempt to resolve via standard payment troubleshooting.
2. Immediately place a temporary hold on the account to prevent further
   transactions.
3. Escalate to the Fraud & Security team within 1 hour.
4. Advise the customer to also contact their bank/card issuer.
5. Do not disclose specific internal fraud-detection methods to the
   customer.

## Q: A customer reports their account was taken over (email/password
changed without their knowledge).

A: This is a security incident, not a standard account-recovery request.
1. Escalate immediately to Fraud & Security.
2. Do not unlock or modify the account via the standard SOP-ACC-002
   process; account takeovers require identity verification through the
   dedicated security team.
3. Advise the customer to change passwords on any other services that
   shared the same password.

## Q: How is severity determined for fraud-related complaints?

A: All confirmed or plausible fraud/unauthorized-transaction/account-
takeover reports are treated as CRITICAL severity by default, regardless
of the monetary amount involved, due to security and trust implications.
