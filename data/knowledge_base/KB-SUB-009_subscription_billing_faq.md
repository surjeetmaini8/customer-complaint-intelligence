## Q: I was charged for a renewal after cancelling.

A: Cancellations must be submitted at least 24 hours before the renewal
date to take effect for that billing cycle; cancellations submitted after
this cutoff will still process one final renewal charge, which should be
disclosed to the customer, with a full refund offered as a goodwill
exception for first-time occurrences.

## Q: My billed amount doesn't match the plan price.

A: Verify whether the customer is on a promotional/discounted plan that
has expired, or if regional tax was applied. Cross-check the plan ID in
the billing system against the customer's stated expectation.

## Q: I can't cancel my subscription - the button doesn't work.

A: Check for any active reported issue with the subscription management
page (see Known Bugs log) before assuming user error. If no known issue,
manually process the cancellation on the customer's behalf and confirm via
email.
